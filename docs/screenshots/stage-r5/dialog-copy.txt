They keep their record but lose access.
A suspended member keeps their record but has no access and no authority until restored.

=== Remove dialog
Remove Carol?
This ends their membership.
The membership ends and the member loses all access to this organization.
They are removed from every session still being configured; each such session's calculation and review is invalidated.
Removal is refused if they are a participant with active obligations (void those first) or a participant of a session that is no longer being configured.
The last active administrator cannot be removed.
Their earlier activity stays on record.

=== Mark ready
Mark this session ready?
Every readiness check passes. Marking it ready records that the application records are consistent.
After this, participants and obligations cannot be changed unless an administrator reopens the session. Ready is not approval, signing or settlement.
CANCEL

=== Reopen session
Reopen this session?
Reopening makes it possible to change a session that was marked ready. Read what that means before you confirm.
WHAT CHANGES
The session becomes "Being configured" again. Participants and obligations can change, and netting can be run again.
WHAT IS KEPT
Reopening alone deletes nothing and changes no record: the calculation, every review decision and any prepared settlement stay exactly as they are.
WHAT STOPS APPLYING LATER
The first change you make afterwards (adding or removing a participant, recording or voiding an obligation, or running netting) makes every review decision stop applying and the calculation stale. A prepared settlement then no longer applies either.
Nothing is erased. Old decisions and the old settlement stay on record, marked "No longer applies". You then calculate, review, prepare and review again, and mark the session ready again.
