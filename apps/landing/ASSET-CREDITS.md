what is **not** known. Nothing here is a licence grant. Where a source or licence could not be verified it says
so; the maintainers need to confirm those before a public release.

What "verified" means below: confirmed from a file in this repository, from the file's own metadata, or by
inspecting the pixels. A name inferred from a filename is labelled as inferred.

## Edits made to the shipped media

- **Hero video:** slowed to 70% speed, frames blended up to 30 fps, played forward then in reverse, and re-encoded without audio. **Seven rooftop signs were blurred** in the encode (the Sheraton, W, Hyatt Regency and ibis Styles hotel signs, the "The One" building name and two Santos signs), so that no real-world brand name is readable in the video. The blurred areas show as soft grey patches.
- **Closing photo:** two vehicle licence plates and a car-brand emblem were blurred.
- **Trust photo:** the text on the projection screens (names, handles, web address, talk and event titles) was blurred and a few logos on the screen edges were softened. The faces of the audience are small and not retouched.

## Replaced and not shipped

- A lower-Manhattan skyline photograph (`trust-skyline.jpg`, "Hauke", Pexels, inferred) was used for the trust section for a time and has been removed; the event photo above replaced it at the founder's request.
- The earlier hero still `hero-poster.jpg` was removed because nothing referenced it.
- Source images (original downloads, background-removed PNGs and the sources used for experiments) live in `apps/landing/assets-src/`, which is git-ignored and is never committed.

1. Confirm the source page, creator and licence for the hero video, the trust photo and the closing photo (the trust photo shows an audience; confirm it is cleared for this use), and add the URLs here. Until then, treat all three as unverified.
2. Record who made the five "How it works" illustrations and under what terms they may be published.
3. Decide whether the blurred sign patches in the hero video are acceptable, or whether a different clip is preferred.
4. Confirm that the new T mark is accepted as the project's brand, and how its authorship should be recorded.
