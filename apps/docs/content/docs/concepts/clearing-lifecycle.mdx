title: Clearing lifecycle
description: From recorded obligations to prepared settlement, and from there to the contract's four session states.
---

## Application workflow

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Draft | A clearing session is created as a local draft. Nothing is on chain. |
| 2. Participants and obligations | Participants are added; each obligation is a debtor, a creditor and an integer amount in one asset. |
| 3. Calculation | The netting engine produces net positions as an immutable snapshot. Any change to participants or obligations makes the earlier result stale. |
| 4. Institutional review | A reviewer approves or rejects **that exact calculation**. Nobody can decide on a result they recorded, changed or calculated. |
| 5. Settlement preparation | An approved calculation becomes an immutable batch of generated instructions; a second review approves the batch. Every participant needs a verified Stellar account on the right network before execution is allowed. |
| 6. Execution (owner-run) | The accounts authorize their own steps and the contract settles. |
| 7. Confirmation and reconciliation (owner-run) | The result is read back by hash and compared with the approved plan. |

Preparation moves no funds. A prepared batch is not a submitted transaction.

## Contract states

```text
OPEN -> COMMITTED -> APPROVED -> SETTLED
```

- **OPEN**: participants join; debtors submit obligations.
- **COMMITTED**: obligations are frozen and their commitment stored; a settlement proposal may be made and replaced.
- **APPROVED**: every admitted participant has approved the current proposal digest.
- **SETTLED**: `settle` executed atomically. It happens once.

Replacing a proposal in COMMITTED or APPROVED clears all approvals atomically and returns the session to COMMITTED.
An approval is bound to a digest, so a stale approval cannot be counted against a different plan.

## Special case: all-zero nets

When every net position is zero, the session can still finalize. The proposal is the empty plan, everyone approves, and
`settle` makes no token call and records SETTLED with zero value.

## What is deliberately absent

There are no timeouts, abort, default handling, recovery, collateral or carry-over, and no privileged administrator or
override. A session may stall indefinitely. These belong to the broader protocol design and are not implemented.
